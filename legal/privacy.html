Who we are

Kombra Group is the parent company of the Kombra Group family. You can reach us at hello@kombragroup.com.

Our registered legal name and postal address will be printed here before this page takes effect.

What we collect

  • email messages you send us

How we use it

  • to provide what you asked for (an order, an account, a reply, a certificate)
  • to keep the site safe and working

Cookies

We use only the cookies and browser storage the site needs to work (for example, keeping you signed in, holding a site password while the site is in preview, and remembering your cookie choice).

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this site.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run the site for us:

  • Vercel (hosting; keeps short-term server logs such as IP address and browser type to run and protect the site)

The Kombra Group family

Kombra Group companies share a name and a family mark. We do not pass your information from one Kombra Group company to another unless you ask us to.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the reason you gave it, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information: email hello@kombragroup.com.
  • Nevada residents: you may send a verified request that we not sell your covered information (NRS 603A.345). We do not sell it; we will confirm that in writing on request.

Children

This site is for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (16 CFR 312.2). If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we delete it.

Security

We protect information with access controls, encryption in transit, and least-privilege access for our staff and tools.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so on this page and, where we have your email, tell you before the change takes effect.
