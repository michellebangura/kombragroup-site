The Meaning of "Kombra"

In the Krio and Mende languages of Sierra Leone, Kombra refers to the mother or caregiver — the one who carries, protects, and nurtures life through vulnerability into strength.

In systems architecture, Kombra represents the steward who carries complex human and organizational systems through chaos until they achieve durable, autonomous maturity.
