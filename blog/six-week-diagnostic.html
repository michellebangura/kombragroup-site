What a 6-Week Diagnostic Actually Produces

Kombra Group · April 2026 · 3 min read

Clients ask: "What do I get for $40,000 in six weeks?"

Here's the answer, deliverable by deliverable.

Week 1-2: We embed with your team. We shadow live sales calls. We interview every rep, every solutions engineer, every customer success manager who touches a deal. We audit your collateral — pitch decks, demo scripts, battlecards, pricing documents, RFP responses. We map your sales process end-to-end, from lead source through close.

We deliver a Current State Assessment (10-15 pages), a Friction Map that identifies every point in the process where deals lose momentum, and a Competitive Intelligence Audit that documents what your team knows about the incumbent versus what they should know.

Week 3-4: We build the architecture. The enterprise sales framework with qualification criteria, discovery methodology, proof-of-value structure, and procurement navigation. The competitive displacement playbook — 40-60 pages of strategy, organized stage by stage. A discovery question bank with 100+ diagnostic questions. A demo choreography script that shows outcomes, not features. An ICP scoring matrix.

Week 5: We build the enablement toolkit. Eight competitive battlecards — one per objection scenario, each with the customer's exact words, the fear underneath, the data point that shifts the conversation, and the proof point that closes the gap. Five buyer persona profiles with objection response matrices. An AI-native sales process map that shows where AI accelerates every stage.

Week 6: We hand it over. A 3-hour team enablement session, recorded for future hires. A 90-day execution plan. A measurement dashboard with baseline metrics and targets. And a hiring recommendation — the profile of the sales leader you should hire to execute the playbook.

Sixteen deliverables. Six weeks. One fixed fee. The playbook pays for itself with a single deal that would have otherwise been lost to the incumbent.
