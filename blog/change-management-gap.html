The Change Management Gap in Enterprise AI

Kombra Group · April 2026 · 3 min read

Enterprise AI vendors have a product problem they don't know about. The product works. The deployment doesn't.

Here's the pattern: the vendor sells a CIO on an AI-powered platform. The implementation goes smoothly. The system is live. And six months later, adoption is at 30% because the 1,175 people who are supposed to use the system every day were never told what their job means now.

The CIO bought AI transformation. The IT team deployed AI infrastructure. Nobody addressed the human layer: the workforce that woke up one morning and discovered that the tool they used to do their job was replaced by something they don't understand, weren't trained on, and didn't ask for.

This is the change management gap. And it's killing enterprise AI deployments.

At Kombra Group, we build the change management layer that most AI vendors don't provide. Through Purpose Built Press, we publish structured workbooks for every role affected by an enterprise AI deployment: the front-line employee figuring out what they're still here to do, the manager leading a team through a transformation they didn't design, and the implementation partner deploying technology into a human organization.

These aren't training manuals. They're self-guided workbooks that help each person articulate their role, understand their value, and produce a signed document that represents their commitment to the new system.

Cost per employee: $13-25. Time to deploy: same day as the system.

The technology is the easy part. The people are the deployment.
