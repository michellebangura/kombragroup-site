Why SAM.gov Registration Matters for Enterprise AI Companies

Kombra Group · April 2026 · 3 min read

If you're selling AI to enterprises and you're not SAM.gov registered, you're leaving the largest buyer in the world off your prospect list.

The U.S. federal government is the single largest purchaser of technology services on the planet. Federal agencies are actively seeking AI solutions for everything from IT service management to fraud detection to citizen services. The mandates are public, the budgets are allocated, and the procurement vehicles exist.

But you can't sell to them without SAM.gov registration.

Kombra Group is a Delaware C-Corp, SAM.gov registered, and federal procurement ready. Here's why that matters for our clients:

First, it signals operational maturity. SAM.gov registration requires entity verification, CAGE code assignment, and compliance with federal acquisition regulations. Companies that complete this process demonstrate the governance infrastructure that enterprise buyers (not just federal ones) evaluate.

Second, it opens doors. Government technology contracts range from $50K task orders to $500M+ indefinite-delivery contracts. If your AI platform is solving the same problems the federal government is trying to solve, the procurement pathway exists. You just need a partner who knows how to navigate it.

Third, the compliance frameworks required for federal work (FedRAMP, CJIS, HIPAA, SOC2) are the same frameworks that give enterprise CIOs confidence. If you can pass a federal security review, you can pass any enterprise procurement evaluation.

We help AI companies navigate this pathway: SAM.gov registration assistance, compliance mapping, RFP response strategy, and proposal governance. 100% shortlist rate across two firms over 6 years. The government buying process is complex but repeatable. We know the playbook.
