Investors and partners

Every Kombra venture has a page for investors and a page for partners on the Kombra Labs site. Start here.

Private preview. Nothing on this page is an offer of securities, a promise of returns, or a statement that any investor, customer or partner has committed. Michelle Bangura has not set investment terms for any venture. Prices marked as hers come from her files; every other number is an assumption, labelled where it appears.

For investors

For each venture: the customer outcome, how to use and prioritise investment (what to buy, what to scale and why), labelled scenarios with their assumptions, how to build the audience, and the path to the first 5, 10 and 100 paying customers, direct and enterprise.

For partners

For each venture: the partners we want to recruit, what each partner gets, the recruitment page, an outreach email draft and the plan for the first 5, 10 and 100 partners. Organisations named there are targets, not partners.
